Double quotes in the text (that is, not in the notes) were encoded as quote-elements. Single quotes, as q-elements.
In the book the text was printed on the left page and the notes on the right pages. The notes were numbered by page an line. In this file the notes were encoded as note-elements with their corresponding n-attribute and are located either after the item(s) they refer or at the end of the line, when they do not refer to a specific word or syllable.
In the notes, variant readings quotations from other texts or the Tarkarahasya itself were enclosed in a q-element with their corresponding xml:lang-attribute.

The text is divided in 6 parts, encoded as div-elements.

tarkarahasye svaparapramāṇasaṅkhyānyāyanirṇayaḥ
TP1/ namo buddhāya|
TL1/ yaḥ śrīmān avadātadhiḥ paraparāmarśācalasyāśaniḥ
TL2/ sadgranthaprathamānapauruṣatayā prāmāṇika…|
TL3/ … dharmatā
TL4/ tasyaitat pratiracyate tanudhiyāṃ ratyai rahasyaṃ mayā||
TL5/ tarkarahasye vimṛśati saṃśayaśaraṇāni śāstravacanāni|
TL6/ …||
TP2/ arthānarthaprāptiparityāgayor avaśyaṃ pramāṇapūrvakatvāt tadaviduṣāṃ tadvyutpādanārtham idam ārabhyate.
TP3/ …yā vipratipattayaḥ saṅkhyālakṣaṇagocaraphala-viṣayās tatpratiṣedhena pramāṇaṃ pratipādyate.
TL7/ pramāṇam avisaṃvādi jñānam arthakriyāsthitiḥ|
TP4/ …na tat pramāṇam. karaṇasādhitena pramāṇaśabdena kartṛkarmakriyākṣepapūrvakaṃ sādhakatama-…yaḥ prādhānyena. tatsahacaraḥ śarīrendriyagaṇas tat…te. karma cārthakriyā…yāpi pramitiḥ sāpi saṃjñāprāyāḥ kṛto bhavantīti nyāyāt pravṛttiviṣayaniyatārthakriyāsamartha…pravṛttyapradarśitam arthaṃ prāptukāmān vyavahartṝn prati nirarthakatvāt. kiṃ hi teṣāṃ yadi pramāṇenārthaḥ pratītaḥ. sā cet pratītis teṣāṃ vāñchābhāji tasminn arthe pravṛttiprāptyor aṅgam, evaṃ tarhi tathaiva teṣāṃ tad upayujyate tathaiva …tathaiva tad vyutpādyatām. teṣāṃ ca vyavahartṝṇām upayujyate paryeṣaṇāpātraṃ ca pramāṇaṃ paricchede 'pi pravṛttiprāptyaṅgatayaiva. na ca paradarśane tathā vyutpāditam. vyutpāditaṃ cācāryeṇa tathā avisaṃvādīti.
TP5/ arthakriyāsthitir avisaṃvādanam| artho dāhādir eṣaṇīyatvāt. tasya yā kriyā niṣpattiḥ karmasthā, tasyāḥ sthitir avicalanam. svapnanirāsārtham. svapne prātaḥ prabuddhasya jhaṭity amlānabhāsanam| tadyogād avisaṃvādīti matvarthīyena, avisaṃvādiśabdaḥ puruṣasya heyopādeyayoḥ pravṛttyapākārayor arthakriyātatsādhanarūpayoḥ prasiddhaḥ, iti bhāṣyamatam.
TP6/ dharmottaramate tu: yatrāvisaṃvādipadaṃ nāsti tatrāpi pradarśitārthaprāpakaḥ prasiddho yathā asmākam ayam avisaṃvādakaḥ puruṣaḥ pramāṇam iti vadanti vyavahartāraḥ. yady api prāpakatvaṃ pramāṇaśabdāt prāptaṃ saṃjñāprāyatvena kṛtāṃ yatrāvisaṃvādipadaṃ nāsti tatrāpy avisaṃvādipadenāpy upāttaṃ vārttike, pramāṇaśabdapravṛttinimittakhyātyartham. bhāṣyamate tv avisaṃvādipadaṃ lakṣaṇasya laukikatvārtham.
TP7/ arthakriyārthī hi sarvaḥ pramāṇam apramāṇaṃ vānveṣate prekṣāvān, na vyasanitayā. tasmān nārthapratītimātraṃ pramitiḥ, kiṃ tarhi yathoktā viśiṣṭaiva. tasyāś ca sādhakatamaṃ nendriyādikaṃ, vakṣyamāṇayuktyā. kiṃ tarhi. jñānagatam arthasārūpyam, atas tadātmakatayā jñānam eva pramāṇam, nendriyādikam. pratyakṣānumānaprathamakṣaṇabhāvi jñānam ākāraviśeṣaniyamenātmaśaktyutpāditaṃ tac ca vyavahartṛvyavasāyavaśenātadrūpaparāvṛttārthakriyāsamarthaṃ paricchindat pravṛtteḥ kāraṇaṃ bhavat prāpter abhyupāyatāṃ spṛśati.
TP8/ na hy upadarśanavyāpārāt pravartakatvam anyad asya. pravartakatvāc ca nāparaṃ prāpakatvam. na hi haste gṛhītvā jñānaṃ pravartayati. na vā puruṣadeśam artham ānayan nayad vā haṭhena puruṣam arthadeśam arthaṃ prāpayati. kiṃ tu samartham arthakriyāyāṃ darśayad eva pravartayati pravṛttiviṣayam upadarśayad eva ca prāpayati. sarveṣām eva ca jñānānām ekaiveyaṃ vidhā, yad utārthopadarśanaṃ nāma. tatas tadanyad aśeṣam adhigatārthādhigantṛtvenāpramāṇam.
TP9/ nanu cārthopadarśanād yadi nāparaṃ pravartakatvādi tad evaṃ punaruktam āvarteta pradarśayat pravartayati pravṛttiviṣayam upadarśayat prāpayatīti ca. yata evaṃ vākyārthaḥ syād upadarśayad upadarśayatīti.
TP10/ na samīcīnam ālocitaṃ pareṇa. pravṛttir nāma vakṣyamāṇalakṣaṇā bhinnaivopadarśanād anyaiva ca prāptir hastādisparśarūpā. param etāvad ucyate: jñānam upadarśayad eva tasyāḥ pravṛtteḥ prāpteś ca nibandhanam, na vyāpārāntareṇa.
TP11/ yathā dvāḥsthavadhyaghātakapṛthvīnātheṣu bahuṣu mārakeṣu kasya cit kathañcid upayogād rājño mārakatvam ājñādānam evety uktau kim ājñādānam ājñādānam ity evāhuḥ, kiṃ vā maraṇaṃ nāma vadhyasya. bhinnam evāste taddhetutvena varṇyamānam ājñādānaṃ vyāpārāntaratiraskāre caritārthaṃ cānyad eveti.
TP12/ tathehāpi veditavyam. bahuṣu prāpakeṣu jñānapādārthaprabhṛtiṣu jñānaṃ kathaṃ prāpayati iti praśne prativacanam upadarśayad eveti, na paunaruktyam.
TP13/ nanu bahuṣu sthāneṣu yad etad uktam arthakriyāsamarthasya prāpaṇayogyam iti, tat katham avadhāryam. kim arthasyopādeyasya dāhādeḥ kriyā niṣpattis tatra samarthasyeti. tathārthatve heyārthakriyāsamarthasyāsaṃgrahān nyūnatā. arthakriyāviṣayasya ca jñānasya prāmāṇyam anupāttaṃ syāt. na hi pradarśitārthakriyāsamarthaprāpaṇayogyaṃ jñānaṃ pramāṇam iti vadatārthakriyāprāpakam api pramāṇamātraṃ bhavati.
TP14/ athārthyata ity artho hātum upādātuṃ cārthyamāno dāhādir evocyate. arthakriyā cārthakriyāsamarthaś ceti virūpaikaśeṣo vivakṣitaḥ. tathāpy ekavacanānupapattiś ca. kathaṃ ca prāpaṇayogyaṃ pramāṇam ity uktau sākṣādarthakriyāprāpakam saṃgṛhyeta. satyam. ayam evārtho hṛdisthaḥ, arthakriyāsamarthasyety upalakṣaṇārtham uktam. tenārthakriyāyā aprāpakam api prāpaṇayogyaṃ veditavyam.
TP15/ abhilāpasaṃsargayogyapratibhāsā pratītiḥ kalpanety atra yathābhilāpasaṃsṛṣṭam asaṃsṛṣṭam api saṃsraṣṭuṃ yogyaṃ yogyaśabdena saṃgṛhyate tathehāpi vyavasthāpyam. yat prāpaṇayogyaṃ yac ca sākṣāt prāpakaṃ tad ubhayaṃ yogyaśabdena viṣayīkartavyam.
TP16/ svasaṃvedanapratyakṣasya cātmānaṃ meyaṃ prati prāpakatvaṃ sātiśayam astīti sāmānyalakṣaṇam akṣīṇam asminn iti veditavyam. viṣayasārūpyam api tasya tādātmye sati sūpapādam. uttaraprāptihetutvāt pūrvasya yadi mānatā| tadaivāsyāpi rūpatvād uttarasya kathaṃ na sā|| tena svataḥ sarvapramāṇānāṃ prāmāṇyam iti gṛhyatām| na hi svato 'satī śaktiḥ kartum anyena pāryate|| catvāro hi pakṣāḥ: 1 kadācit prāmāṇyam aprāmāṇyaṃ ca svataḥ. 2 kiṃ vā svato 'prāmāṇyaṃ parataḥ prāmāṇyam. 3 athavā dvayam api parataḥ. 4 kiṃ vā svataḥ prāmāṇyam aprāmāṇyaṃ parataḥ. tatra: 1 parasparaviruddhadvayaprasaṅgān nādyaḥ. 2 anupalambhena prāmāṇyapratiṣedhasya sambhāvyatvān na dvitīyaḥ. 3 ato 'nupalambhena yady apy aprāmāṇyaṃ tathāpi prāmāṇyam api saṃvādaguṇavijñānād yadi parataḥ, tadā-saṃvādaguṇavijñāne kena vābhyadhike mate| sāpekṣatve pramāṇatvaṃ na vyavasthāpyate kvacit|| iti nyāyān na tṛtīyaḥ. 4 tasmāt svataḥpramāṇatvam utsargena vyavasthitaṃ| bādhakāraṇaduṣṭatvajñānābhyāṃ tad apodyate|| iti nyāyāt svataḥ prāmāṇyam aprāmāṇyam anyata iti nyāyyam.
TP17/ ity apāstam. sāpekṣam eva prāmāṇyam, na cānavasthā. kāraṇajñāne hi tadutpattisambandhenāvisaṃvāditvam, sākṣāt prāpake tādātmyena. tato vyavatiṣṭhata eva prāmāṇyam. tena saṃvādenāpi saṃvādaḥ punar mṛgyas tathaiva hi| ity anavasthā pratyākhyātā.
TP18/ nanu saṃvādo nāmottarārthajñānajñeyarūpaḥ sa kiṃ 1sajātīyo 2vijātīyo vā. 1yady ādyas tadā vedajñānaṃ vedajñānāntarasaṃvādāt pramāṇaṃ syāt. 2atha dvitīyas tad api śabdadhīś cāpramāṇaṃ syād itarābhir asaṅgateḥ| iti prāptam.
TP19/ ghaṭajñānaṃ paṭajñānāt pramāṇaṃ kiṃ na kīrtyate| iti ca syāt. na. sambaddhārthapariprāptyā pramāṇaṃ sarvam ucyate| na sajātivijātīyavijñānotpattimātrataḥ|| matvarthīyena niṣpannam avisaṃvādi vedyatām| tadutpatteś ca tādātmyasambandhāt tasya sambhavaḥ|| tena-śābde 'py abhiprāyanivedanāt. śabdo vipañcīpañcamādidhvaniḥ, tasyedaṃ jñānaṃ śābdam, tasmin. apiśabdāt pāvakādijñāne. arthakriyāsthitir avisaṃvādanam iti prakṛtena sambandhaḥ. katham. abhiprāyanivedanāt. iṣṭatvāniṣṭatvābhyāṃ puruṣasyārthaparicchedo 'bhiprāyaḥ, tena niṣṭhitaṃ vedanaṃ nivedanam. idaṃ vedyamānam arthakriyāyāḥ.
TP20/ tathā hi: arthakriyāsamartham iti jñāne 'rthakriyānivedanam, arthakriyānubhave tu sutarām abhiprāyanivedanam. meyasvarūpasaṃvittir eva tatra kriyā matā| citre 'pi dṛṣṭimātreṇa phalaṃ parisamāptimat|| airāvatīcandrasūryagraharāśyādivedane| abhiprāyāvisaṃvādāt prāmāṇyaṃ nyāyavij jagau|| vede sambandhavaidhuryāt sajātīye 'pi na prāmāṇyaṃ gṛhyatām. sabhyāḥ śaktivādo hy anādṛtaḥ.
TP21/ yat punar nārtham ānayi tan na samartham, dravyaguṇakarmasāmānyasamavāyāntyaviśeṣaṃ yathāsamayam ākhyātam, tajjñānaṃ tadviṣayatayāpūrvārtham api na pramāṇam, vyavahartṝn prati nirarthakatvāt. arthakriyāsamarthasya vicāraiḥ kiṃ tadarthinām| ṣaṇḍhasya rūpavairūpye kāminyāḥ kiṃ parīkṣayā|| iti nyāyāt.

TP54/ tathā hi śabdajñānād asannikṛṣṭe 'rthe jñānaṃ śābdam. śabdasvalakṣaṇagrahaṇād uttarakālaṃ parokṣe 'rthe yad utpadyate jñānaṃ tat śabdād āgatam iti kṛtvā śābdaṃ pramāṇam. tac ca dvividham apauruṣeyaśabdajanitaṃ pratyayitapuruṣavākyajaṃ ca. āha ca: śabdād udeti yaj jñānam apratyakṣe 'rthavastuni| yad akartṛkato vākyād yad vā pratyayitoditāt|| na jñānam idam adhyakṣaṃ parokṣaviṣayatvataḥ| nānumānaṃ ca ghaṭate trairūpyeṇa viyogataḥ|| dharmī dharmaviśiṣṭo hi liṅgīty etat suniścitam| na tāvad anumānaṃ ca yāvat tadviṣayaṃ na tat|| yaś cātra kalpyate dharmī prameyo 'sya sa eva hi| na cānavadhṛte tasmiṃs taddharmasyāvadhāraṇā|| prāk sacet pakṣadharmatvād gṛhītaḥ kiṃ tataḥ param| pakṣadharmādibhir jñātair yena syād anumānatā|| anvayo na ca śabdasya prameyeṇa nirūpyate| vyāpāreṇa hi sarveṣām anvetṛtvaṃ pratīyate|| yatra dhūmo 'sti tatrāgner astitvenānvayaḥ sphuṭaḥ| na tv eva yatra śabdo 'sti tatrārtho 'stīti niścayaḥ|| na tāvat tatra deśe 'sau na tatkāle ca gamyate| tasmāc ca śabdaliṅgasya prameyeṇānvayāgatiḥ| bhaven nityavibhutvāc cet sarvaśabdeṣu tat samam|| tena sarvatra dṛṣṭatvād vyatirekasya cāgatiḥ| sarvaśabdair aśeṣārthapratipattiḥ prasajyate|| tasmād ananumānatvaṃ śābde pratyakṣavad bhavet| trairūpyarahitatvena tādṛgviṣayavarjanāt|| agnihotrādivacanād akampajñānajanmataḥ| tatpramāṇatvam apy asya nirākartuṃ na pāryate|| iti.
TP55/ parokṣārthe sādṛśyaparicchedas tūpamānam. tac ca dvividham eva sādṛśyātmakaṃ jñānātmakaṃ ca. tad atra paridṛśyamānagavayagatasādṛśyaṃ vastubhūtam. sādṛśyasya hi vastutvaṃ na śakyam apavāditum| bhūyo'vayavasāmānyayogo jātyantarasya tat|| tasmād yat smaryate tat syāt sādṛśyena viśeṣitam| prameyam upamānasya sādṛśyaṃ vā tadāśritam|| gāṃ dṛṣṭvāyam araṇyānyāṃ gavayaṃ vīkṣate yadā| bhūyo'vayavasāmānyabhājaṃ vartulakaṇṭhakam|| tadāsya gavaye 'jñātarūpamātrāvabodhakam| pratyakṣam eva yac cāpi viśeṣeṇa vikalpakam|| gavā sadṛśarūpo 'yaṃ paśur ity etad īdṛśam| akṣavyāpārasadbhāve jāteḥ pratyakṣam iṣyate|| tatra yady api gāṃ smṛtvā tajjñānam upajāyate| sannidher gavayasthatvād bhaved indriyagocaraḥ|| sāmānyaṃ ca dvisambaddhaṃ pratyekaṃ ca samāpyate| pratiyoginy adṛṣṭe 'pi yasmāt tad upalabhyate|| pratyakṣeṇāvabuddhe 'pi sādṛśye gavi ca smṛte| viśiṣṭasyānyato 'siddher upamāyāḥ pramāṇatā|| pratyakṣe 'pi yathā deśe smaryamāṇe ca pāvake| viśiṣṭaviṣayatvena nānumānāpramāṇatā|| na hi pratyakṣatāpy asya vijñānasyopapadyate| indriyārthābhisambandhavyāpāravirahāt tadā|| trairūpyānupapatteś ca na tasyāpy anumānatā| pakṣadharmādi naivātra kathañcid api yujyate|| prāk prameyasya sādṛśyaṃ na dharmatvena gṛhyate| gavaye gṛhyamāṇaṃ ca na gavām anumāpakam|| pratijñārthaikadeśatvād gogatasya na liṅgatā| gavayaś cāpy asambandhān na goliṅgatvam ṛcchati|| na cāpramāṇaṃ tajjñānam ajñātārthaprakāśanāt| gavayālokanāt pūrvaṃ tatsādṛśyānavagrahāt|| iti.
TP56/ prasiddhasādharmyāt sādhyasādhanam upamānam iti. prasiddhena gavā sādharmyaṃ sādṛśyalakṣaṇaṃ yasya sa prasiddhasādharmyo gavayaḥ. tasmād iti pañcamī. tam eva hetūkṛtya sādhyo yaḥ saṃjñāsaṃjñisambandhas tasya sādhanaṃ siddhir yā pratipattiḥ sopamānam iti.
TP57/ anenaitad uktaṃ bhavati: gaur iva gavaya iti vṛddhasyātideśavākyasya śravaṇād anena gavā sadṛśe prāṇini parokṣe 'viśeṣato gavayasambandho yena prathamaṃ pratipannaḥ syāt. paścād araṇyaṃ gatvā gavayadarśanād vṛddhātideśavākyaṃ smṛtvā caivaṃ sa pratipadyate: ayam eva sa prāṇī gavayaśabdavācyo yo mayā pūrvam aviśeṣato gavayasaṃjñakaḥ pratipanna iti. pūrvaṃ hy aviśeṣataḥ saṃjñāsambandhapratipattir āsīt. idānīṃ tayā vyaktiviśeṣaviṣayaniṣṭhā saṃjñāsaṃjñisambandhapratipattiḥ prajāyate, sopamānaṃ pramāṇāntaram.
TP58/ anye punar amum evārtham āhuḥ: āgamāhitasaṃskārasmṛtyapekṣāt sādharmyajñānāt samākhyāsambandhapratipattir upamānam. gaur iva gavaya ity atideśavākyam āgamaḥ. tenāhito jñānajo jñānahetuś cety evaṃlakṣaṇo yaḥ saṃskārākhyo guṇaḥ, tato yātideśavākyāt smṛtir upajāyate 'raṇye gavayadarśanāt, tām apekṣate yat sādharmyajñānaṃ tat tathoktam. samākhyā saṃjñāśabda iti yāvat. tasyāḥ sahārthena yaḥ sambandhas tasya pratipattir upamānam. āha ca: śrutātideśavākyasya samānārthopalambhane| saṃjñāsambandhavijñānam upamā kaiścid iṣyate|| iti.
TP59/ dṛṣṭaḥ śruto vārtho 'nyathā nopapadyata ity adṛṣṭakalpanārthāpattiḥ. pramāṇaṣaṭkavijñāto yatrārtho nānyathā bhavan| adṛṣṭaṃ kalpayed anyaṃ sārthāpattir udāhṛtā|| tatra pratyakṣato jñātād dāhād dahanaśaktatā| vahner anumitāt sūrye yānāt tacchaktiyogitā|| śaktayaḥ sarvabhāvānāṃ kāryārthāpattisādhanāḥ| apūrvās tāś ca gamyante sambandhagrahaṇād ṛte|| na cāsāṃ pūrvasambandho na cānyo gṛhyate 'dhunā| kāryaiḥ saha yataḥ syātāṃ pakṣadharmānvayāv iha|| śrotrādiśaktipakṣe vā yāvān hetuḥ prayujyate| sarvo 'sāv āśrayāsiddho dharmyasiddheḥ prayujyate|| pīno divā na bhuṅkte cety evamādivacaḥśrutau| rātribhojanavijñānaṃ śrutārthāpattir ucyate|| na rātryādipadārthaś ca divāvākye 'vagamyate| na divādipadārthānāṃ saṃsargo rātribhojanam| na bhedo yena tad vākyaṃ tasya syāt pratipādakam| anyārthavyāpṛtatvāc ca na dvitīyārthakalpanā| tasmād vākyāntareṇāyaṃ buddhisthena pratīyate| tenānāgamikatve 'pi yat tad vākyaṃ pratīyate| pramāṇaṃ tasya vaktavyaṃ pratyakṣādiṣu yad bhavet| na tv anuccārite vākye pratyakṣaṃ tāvad iṣyate| nānumānaṃ na hīdaṃ hi dṛṣṭaṃ tena saha kvacit| yadi tv anupalabdhe 'pi sambandhe liṅgam iṣyate| taduccāraṇamātreṇa sarvavākyamatir bhavet| sambandharahitatvena nānyatas tad viśiṣyate| gavayopamitā yā gaus tajjñānagrāhyaśaktatā| upamābalasambhūtasāmarthyena pratīyate| abhidhā nānyathā siddhyed iti vācakaśaktatām| arthāpattyāvagamyaiva tadananyagateḥ punaḥ| arthāpattyantareṇaiva śabdanityatvaniścayaḥ| anityo hi na saṅketavyavahārānuvṛttibhāg| iti.
TP60/ pramāṇābhāvanirṇītacaitrābhāvaviśeṣitāt| gehāc caitrabahirbhāvasiddhir yā tv iha varṇitā|| tām abhāvotthitām anyām arthāpattim udāharet| pakṣadharmādyanaṅgatvād bhinnaiṣāpy anumānataḥ|| bahirdeśaviśiṣṭe 'rthe deśe vā tadviśeṣite| prameye yo gṛhābhāvaḥ pakṣadharmas tv asau katham|| jīvataś ca gṛhābhāvaḥ pakṣadharmo 'tra kalpyate| tatsaṃvittir bahirbhāvaṃ na cābuddhvopajāyate|| gehābhāvas tu yaḥ śuddho vidyamānatvavarjitaḥ| sa mṛteṣv api dṛṣṭatvād bahirvṛtter na sādhakaḥ|| atra catasṛbhiḥ śaktir eva pramīyate. ekayā śabdanityatvam aparayā bahiḥsthitaṃ dravyam eveti piṇḍārthaḥ.
TP61/ pramāṇapañcakaṃ yatra vasturūpe na jāyate| vastusattāvabodhārthaṃ tatrābhāvapramāṇatā|| iha sarvam eva vastu sadasadrūpeṇa dvyātmakam iti.
TL8/ pratyakṣāder anutpattiḥ pramāṇābhāva iṣyate|
TL9/ sātmano 'pariṇāmo vā vijñānaṃ vānyavastuni||
TL10/ pramābhāvāc ca vastūnām abhāvaḥ sampratīyate|
TL11/ caturdhā ca vibhinno 'sau prāgabhāvādibhedataḥ||
TL12/ kṣīre dadhyādi yan nāsti prāgabhāvaḥ sa kathyate|
TL13/ nāstitā payaso dadhni pradhvaṃsābhāvalakṣaṇam||
TL14/ gavi yo 'śvādyabhāvas tu so 'nyonyābhāva ucyate|
TL15/ pararūpaṃ na tasyāsti nāsti tenātmanā tataḥ||
TL16/ śiraso 'vayavā nimnā vṛddhikāṭhinyavarjitāḥ|
TL17/ śaśaśṛṅgādirūpeṇa so 'tyantābhāva ucyate||
TL18/ na ca syād vyavahāro 'yaṃ kāraṇādivibhāgataḥ|
TL19/ prāgabhāvādibhedena nābhāvo bhidyate yadi||
TL20/ na cāvastuna ete syur bhedās tenāsya vastutā|
TL21/ kāryādīnām abhāvaḥ ko bhāvo yaḥ kāraṇādinaḥ||
TL22/ yad vānuvṛttivyāvṛttibuddhigrāhyo yatas tv ayam|
TL23/ tasmād gavādivad vastu prameyatvāc ca gamyatām||
TP62/ etad ayuktam, bhinnaviṣayatvāt. lakṣaṇabhedād dhi bhavantaḥ pramāṇānāṃ nānātvaṃ pratipannāḥ, vayaṃ punar dvaividhyāt prameyasya pramāṇadvaividhyam avabudhyāmahe. tad yadi lakṣaṇabhedān nānātvaṃ ṣoḍhā na syāt prauḍhānām api pramāṇānāṃ vyavasthā. ādyamadhyāntatayā sammatasvabhāveṣu bhāveṣu prathamaṃ dṛṣṭvā dvitīyadarśanād ādyam, prathamāntayor vilokanān madhyam, prathamamadhyayor avadhāraṇād anantaraṃ tṛtīyadarśanād antyavyavasthā. eteṣv api ca yaj jñānam utpadyate tatrāpy evam abhidhātuṃ śakyate: tatrāpūrvārthavijñānaṃ niścitaṃ bādhavarjitam| aduṣṭakāraṇārabdhaṃ pramāṇaṃ lokasammatam|| uktaṃ ca yuktimatā- dṛśyāt parokṣe sādṛśyadhīḥ pramāṇāntaraṃ yadi| vaidharmyamatir apy evaṃ pramāṇaṃ kiṃ na saptamam|| tasmād upekṣyatāṃ lakṣaṇabhedagrahaḥ. prameyabhede tātparye sā varjyatām. arthakriyārthināṃ hi vyavahartṝṇām upayujyamānena mānena pravṛttiviṣayaḥ śakyaprāpaṇo niyato 'rthakriyāsamarthaḥ pratyakṣo 'pratyakṣaś ca pramātavyo viṣayaḥ|
TP63/ tataś ca pratyayitapradiṣṭāt kartṛvidhuritāc ca vākyāt tripramāṇakaśaktilakṣaṇasambandhāt sāmānyātmanā pratipadyamānaḥ parokṣo 'rthas trirūpaliṅgapratipādyaviṣaye satyam antarbhavati. paraṃ kartur āptatvam akartṛtvaṃ tripramāṇakaśaktilakṣaṇasambandhaś ca tīrthasārthaprārthanāsahasrāṇām apy aviṣayaḥ.
TP64/ tathā hy āptatvaṃ duravadhāram. nāvadhānārhaṃ cākartṛkatvam, sakartṛkatvasādhanasambhavāt. śaktivādamayodyaiva ca kāryam arjayitum ūrjasvī yaḥ kaścid bhāva iti pratipādayiṣyate. ity evam āptatvādiviśeṣaṇagaṇe guṇibhir avagaṇite yathāyathaṃ arthair anāntarīyakaḥ śabdarāśir avaśiṣyate. tasmāt tadutthaḥ pratyayaḥ pratibhājñānavad yauktapratyavamarśasvabhāvān na bhramyati. tadupadarśito 'py artho bhāvābhāvayor aniyatatvād aśakyaprāpaṇo na prīṇāti vyavahartṝn.
TP65/ upamānasyāpi viṣayaḥ parokṣasya goḥ sādṛśyaṃ tadupādhir vā gauḥ. ubhayathāpi pūrvapratyakṣadṛṣṭasya goḥ sādṛśyaṃ viśeṣyaṃ viśeṣaṇaṃ vā nārthakriyāsamartho viṣayaḥ, sāmānyasya dūṣayiṣyamāṇatvāt, kāminyāḥ ṣaṇḍharūpavairūpyavat. pākādyarthakriyārthināṃ na prārthanīyaḥ. tataś ca so 'pi na pravṛttiviṣayabhedaḥ san pramāṇabhedam upakalpayati vailakṣaṇyādijñānavat. naiyāyikopamānaṃ tu svabhāvahetāv antaryāti. tathā hi: yaḥ śabdo yatra vṛddhaiḥ prayujyate so 'sati vṛttyantare tasya vācakaḥ, yathā gośabdo gor arthasya, prayujyate caiṣa gavayaśabdo gosadṛśe prāṇinīti tasyaiva vācaka iti jñānam anumānam eva.
TP66/ ṣaṭprakārāpy arthāpattiḥ sambandham anādṛtya pratipādyānubandhinīti varṇitam. kathaṃ cāsambaddho 'rtho 'rthāpattyā boddhavya iti sarvavarṇair eva suciraṃ cintanīyam. pīno devadatto 'kṛtarasāyano divā na bhuṅkta iti vākyaṃ vivakṣāyāṃ jāyate, sā ca kṣapābhakṣaṇavivakṣā sadhrīcīnā satī pīnādivākyaṃ prakalpayati karaṇagaṇapreraṇayā. anyathā pīno 'kṛtarasāyano diveti padatrayam anādeyaṃ syāt. svasantāne caiṣa vivarto vyutpattisamaye sampradhāritaḥ. ataḥ śabdād vivakṣāviśeṣānumitau naktaṃ bhuṅkta iti vākyāntaraṃ sthiram eva vikalpabuddhistham arthāntaraṃ prathayad āste. prayogadarśanābhyāsāt svayam apy evaṃ vyavacchedapratītir bhavatīti nyāyāt. etenānvayavākyād vyatirekavarṇanaṃ vyākhyātam. tena sāmarthyam icchataḥ kīrter naṣṭaṃ dvitvāvadhāraṇam| ity anavadhāraṇād bhaṇitam. evam jīvan gṛha iti padadvayopādānād jīvan devadatto gṛhe nāstīti vivṛtam. śeṣe tu śaktyādau vivādaḥ.
TP67/ abhāvaḥ punar anupalabdhiḥ. tadbhāvabhāvānupalabdhī tarhi prabhavābhāvasādhane nānumānam ananvayād ityādināpi yuktyanupalabdhī carakacarcite vyavahārasādhanād anumānam iti.
TP68/ tad evam samarthaniyataśakyaprāpaṇaprameyapratibandhamātreṇa prāmāṇyamātram atra vyāptam. tadbhedena ca tadbhedaḥ. tato: yaḥ pratyayaḥ samarthaniyataśakyaprāpaṇaprameyapratibaddho na bhavati na sa pramāṇam, tad yathā śaradindukundakumudakamanīyakāntau kambubimbe jāmbūnadadravadigdhadravyapratispardhipītapratibhāsapratyayaḥ, na bhavanti ca vivādādhyāsitāḥ samarthaniyataśakyaprāpaṇaprameyapratibaddhāḥ śabdopamānārthāpattipratyayāḥ.
TP69/ na cāyaṃ vyāpakānupalambho hetuḥ pūrvapratibandhapratipāditapramāṇavṛttyāsiddhyadhyāsitaḥ. nāpi pracaṇḍanaktañcaracakravartinevānaikāntikatvenākrāntapauruṣaḥ. prāmāṇyasya prameyasambaddhatvaṃ vyāpakam anvayavyatirekābhyāṃ nirṇītam. atas tadadhīnaṃ tadapāye kathaṃ śaṅkyeta. nāpi viruddhatayā sārdham ayam arharūpaḥ, sapakṣe vīkṣaṇāt.
TP70/ nanu bhavān arhati dravyadeśīyo vidyāvaidagdhyaprasādaḥ. śābdopamānādi na pramāṇam iti cārvākacarcitam evārciṣmatā carcitam. idaṃ tu nirodhabodhasarvasvam apratyakṣo 'rthaḥ sambaddhād anyasmāt sambandhānubandhinā svabhāvena prameyatām alam ātmasātkarttum. sakalakālakalākalāpavyāpinā ca svabhāvena sambandhāvabodhaḥ kārtsnyaṃ ca deśānām adhigamya prasañjyamāno manorathānām apy aviṣayaḥ. avasthādeśakālānāṃ bhedād bhinnāsu śaktiṣu| bhāvānām anumānena prasiddhir atidurlabhā|| nirjñātaśakter arthasya tāṃ tām arthakriyāṃ prati| viśiṣṭadravyasambandhe sā śaktiḥ pratibadhyate|| yatnenānumito 'py arthaḥ kuśalair anumātṛbhiḥ| abhiyuktatarair anyair anyathaivopapādyate|| hastasparśād ivāndhena viṣame pathi dhāvatā| anumānapradhānena vinipāto na durlabhaḥ|| aho, vidyāprasādaṃ dadatā tvayā dānātirāgād ātmapravṛttiviḍambo 'pi na saṃvṛtaḥ. tathā hi: pratyakṣam evaikaṃ pramāṇam iti bruvatā kasyāñcid jñānavyaktau saṃvādam upalabhya sarvaiva tādṛgvidhā pramāṇam iti paricchinnāparicchinnasaṃvādabuddhisāmānyaviṣayo 'yam upadeśaḥ kṛtaḥ, sa cānumeyatāṃ tādrūpyeṇa nātikrāmati. api ca yasmai prasādo deyaḥ so 'numeyaḥ. tato 'pi na pratyakṣam evaikam iti niyamaḥ.
TP71/ tathā hi visaṃvādā na pramāṇam iti pratiṣedham abhidadhāno 'nupalambham eva sambhāvayasi prāmāṇyena. tad evam: pramāṇetarasāmānyasthiter anyadhiyo gateḥ| pramāṇāntarasadbhāvaḥ pratiṣedhāc ca kasyacit|| iti subhāṣitam.
TP72/ yat punar uktaṃ nirjñātaśakter ityādi tat kim asati vidhurapratyayopanipāta iti viśeṣaṇaṃ subhagaśravaṇa nāśrauṣīḥ. tasmāj janmatanmātrānubandhābhyāṃ svabhāvapratibaddhatvād bodhe 'nāśvāsaḥ parihāryaḥ. idaṃ tu bodhasarvasvaṃ svīkuru. arthasyāsambhave 'bhāvāt pratyakṣe 'pi pramāṇatā| pratibaddhasvabhāvasya taddhetutve samaṃ dvayam|| iti. tatra pratyakṣād anumānasya bhinnapramāṇatve prayogaḥ: yat pratyakṣaviṣayavilakṣaṇaviṣayaṃ na tat pratyakṣam, yathotsūtracchāttravāñchāvikalpaḥ, pratyakṣaviṣayavilakṣaṇaviṣayaṃ cānumānam. nedaṃ sādhyasādhanasaudhaśiraḥśekharīkṛtaṃ kṛtāspadam asiddhipaddhatyā. svataḥ svaviṣayavijñānajananāśakteḥ parato gamakānugasāmānyarūpeṇa nirūpaṇāt. anaikāntikatvenāpi nāntikam upasarpatā sarpeṇeva nirdalitadarpanuktalakṣaṇaprameyasambandhamātreṇa pramāṇatvasāmānyavyāptivat, tadbhedasambandhena tadbhedavyāpteḥ. virodho 'pi nedam adhyavasyaty avagāhitum, sapakṣe vīkṣaṇād iti.

TP82/ dṛśyavikalpyāv arthāv ekīkṛtya vyavahārapravṛtter visaṃvādaś ca. dṛśyaḥ sphuṭatvena gṛhyamāṇaḥ, pūrvatayā pratīyamāno vikalpyaḥ. kaḥ punar asau, ubhayathāpi nīla evaikībhūto 'rthaḥ. sa cāsphuṭo nīlo 'rthakriyāvirahalakṣaṇabādhakapramāṇena. paramārthānyavyāvṛttisvabhāvo 'poha ity ucyate. etena svavāgvirodho 'pi pratyākhyātaḥ. naimittikyāḥ śruter artham arthaṃ vā pāramārthikam| śabdānāṃ pratirundhāno na bādhyas tena varṇitaḥ|| iti nyāyāt. tathaikapratyavamarśaniyame vācakaliṅgasambandhasāmānyasāmānādhikaraṇyādivyavahārā jñātavyāḥ. vicchedaṃ sūcayann ekam apratikṣipya vartate| yadānyaṃ tena sa vyāpta ekatvena ca bhāsate|| sāmānādhikaraṇyaṃ syāt tadā buddhyanurodhataḥ|

TP84/ sāmānyavyavahāravad ghaṭādidravyavyavahāro 'pi jñeyaḥ. teṣām eva ghaṭādīnāṃ sādhāraṇaṃ yat phalam udakāharaṇam, tatra ye hetavo na bhavanti ghaṭasanniveśenāvasthitā rūpādayaḥ, tebhyo vyavacchede ghaṭaśrutiḥ. evaṃ ghaṭādiśrutir api vivṛtā. uktaṃ ca-rūpādiśaktibhedānām anākṣepeṇa vartate| tatsamānaphalāhetuvyavacchede ghaṭaśrutiḥ|| tato na rūpaṃ ghaṭa ity ekādhikaraṇā śrutiḥ| bhedaś cāyam ato jātisamudāyābhidhāyinoḥ|| iti. saṃsthānavarṇāvayavair viśiṣṭe yaḥ pravartate| śabdo na tasyāvayave pravṛttir upalabhyate|| yathā cakracitrāḍhakādayaḥ.

TP260/ yadi bāhyapadārthaprākaṭyānyathānupapattyā svacittānumānam. evaṃ tarhi manobuddheḥ smaraṇarūpayoḥ padārthaprākaṭyaliṅgābhāvān nānumānam. viparītakhyātyā sāmānyapratibhāsanena vāsanānāṃ ca, anubhavānusārān nīrūpatvāc ca sāmānyasya nirastam.
