<B 4>

Viṣam (The Poison)
N20.032a/ ataḥ paraṃ pravakṣyāmi viṣasya vidhim uttamam |
N20.032c/ tulayitvā viṣaṃ pūrvaṃ deyam etad dhimāgame || 32 ||
N20.033a/ na pūrvāhṇe na madhyāhne na sandhyāyāṃ tu dharmavit |
N20.033c/ śaradgrīṣmavasanteṣu varṣāsu ca na dāpayet || 33 ||
N20.034a/ bhagnaṃ ca dāritaṃ caiva dhūpitaṃ miśritaṃ tathā |
N20.034c/ kālakūṭam alambuṃ ca viṣaṃ yatnena varjayet || 34 ||
N20.035a/ śārṅgahaimavataṃ śastaṃ gandhavarṇarasānvitam |
N20.035c/ mahādoṣavate deyaṃ rājñā tattvabubhutsayā || 35 ||
N20.036a/ na bālāturavṛddheṣu naiva svalpāparādhiṣu |
N20.036c/ viṣasya tu yavān sapta dadyāc chodye ghṛtaplutān || 36 ||
N20.037a/ viṣasya palaṣaḍbhāgād bhāgo viṃśatimas tu yaḥ |
N20.037c/ tam aṣṭabhāgahīnaṃ tu śodhye dadyād ghṛtaplutam || 37 ||
N20.038a/ yathoktena vidhānena viprān sprṣṭvānumoditaḥ |
N20.038c/ sopavāsaś ca khādeta devabrāhmaṇasannidhau || 38 ||
N20.039a/ viṣaṃ vegaklamāpetaṃ sukhena yadi jīryate |
N20.039c/ viśuddham iti taṃ jñātvā rājā satkṛtya mokṣayet || 39 ||
N20.040a/ tvaṃ viṣa brahmaṇaḥ putraḥ satyadharmaratau sthitaḥ |
N20.040c/ śodhayainaṃ naraṃ pāpāt satyenāsyāmṛtībhava || 40 ||

Kośaḥ (The Holy Water)
N20.041/ ataḥ paraṃ pravakṣyāmi kośasya vidhim uttamam || 41 ||
N20.042a/ pūrvāhṇe sopavāsasya snātasyārdrapaṭasya ca |
N20.042c/ saśūkasyāvyasaninaḥ kośapānaṃ vidhīyate || 42 ||
N20.043a/ yadbhaktaḥ so 'bhiyuktaḥ syāt taddaivatyaṃ tu pāyayet |
N20.043c/ saptāhād yasya dṛśyate dvisaptāhena vā punaḥ |
N20.043e/ pratyātmikaṃ tu yatkiñcit saiva tasya vibhāvanā || 43 ||
N20.044a/ dvisaptāhāt paraṃ yasya mahad vā vaikṛtaṃ bhavet |
N20.044c/ nābhiyojyaḥ sa viduṣāṃ kṛtakālavyatikramāt || 44 ||
N20.045a/ mahāparādhe nirdharme kṛtaghne klībakutsite |
N20.045c/ nāstikavrātyadāseṣu kośapānaṃ vivarjayet || 45 ||
N20.046a/ yathoktena prakāreṇa pañca divyāni dharmavit |
N20.046c/ dadyād rājābhiyuktānāṃ pretya ceha ca nandati || 46 ||
N20.047a/ na viṣaṃ brāhmaṇe dadyān na lohaṃ kṣatriyo haret |
N20.047c/ na nimajjyāpsu vaiśyaś ca śūdraḥ kośaṃ na pāyayet || 47 ||
N20.048a/ varṣāsu na viṣaṃ dadyāt hemante nāpsu majjayet |
N20.048c/ na lohaṃ hārayed grīṣme na kośaṃ pāyayen niśi || 48 ||
